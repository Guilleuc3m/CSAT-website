CSAT — PTL

GC Director: Guillermo Carpintero

Photonics laboratory at the Science Park

CSAT-PTL is our photonics-focused facility, located at the Leganés Science Park. It specializes in the assembly and packaging of photonic chips, enabling the development of satellite payloads with advanced optical communication capabilities — a service currently unique in Spain.
