About Us

Where to find us

CSAT operates across three UC3M sites in the Madrid region, Leganés campus, Science Park (PTL) and Colmenarejo. Our main facility is located in Leganés campus.

CSAT — Leganés

Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, Escuela Politécnica Superior. Avenida de la Universidad, 30, 28911 Leganés, Madrid.

Our main facility, located at UC3M's Leganés campus in the south of Madrid. Home to our cleanroom, integration labs and mission control center.

How to get here ↗

CSAT — PTL

Parque Tecnológico, Av. Gregorio Peces Barba, 1, 28919 Leganés, Madrid.

Located at the Leganés Science Park, this facility hosts our photonics lab, dedicated to the development of advanced optical communication payloads.

How to get here ↗

CSAT — Colmenarejo

Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, Campus de Colmenarejo. Avenida de la Universidad Carlos III, 22, 28270 Colmenarejo, Madrid.

Located at UC3M's Colmenarejo campus, west of Madrid. This facility houses our advanced radiometry equipment for electromagnetic measurement and satellite payload testing.
