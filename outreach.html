Outreach

This outreach webpage and the educational material #4 to #6 were created with funding by the CITT-Espacio (“Clúster de Innovación Tecnológica y Talento en tecnologías del Espacio”) from the Comunidad de Madrid (CAM).

In addition to our standard responsibilities as academic staff (teaching, supervising students, and performing research and development), the faculty team at the CSAT aims to bring our work to the general public via presentations, workshops, organization of visits (to CSAT or at schools), and Do-It-Yourself (DIY) activities.

Educational material Visits and talks

In this webpage you will find educational material (free to use if properly cited) that we have created, plus a list of the activities we have been involved in the past few years. These activities cover anything that is not a technical presentation at a conference or seminar, and were aimed at presenting our technical activities and space to a non-specialized audience (from the general public and non-engineering university students, up to stakeholders –including members of scientific and/or policy-making organizations).
